🏠 Dashboard/Overview:
Ek general summary jahan main stats (Total Sales, Revenue) nazar aayein.

📦 Products:
Is page par aap products ki list dikha sakte hain (Image, Price, Stock status).

🛒 Orders:
Pending aur Completed orders ki list. Static data mein aap 5-10 fake orders dikha sakte hain.

👥 Customers:
Un users ki list jinhon ne shopping ki hai.

📊 Analytics:
Yahan aap Recharts library use karke sales ka graph dikha sakte hain (static data par bhi graph bohat cool lagta hai).

⚙️ Settings:
Profile update karne ya theme (Dark/Light mode) change karne ke liye.
